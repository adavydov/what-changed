Sources & method

Each issue is built from the full in-period corpus—not a few convenient examples. Publisher fame alone never makes an idea important.

Workflow

Method

  1. 01

    Scan a broad radar

    A broad portfolio helps surface early shifts, but it does not impose quotas, issue topics, or a closed boundary on the research.

  2. 02

    Read strong signals in full

    For a potentially important item, we read the original, methods, appendix, or transcript and look for the causal mechanism beneath the headline.

  3. 03

    Research around the claim

    A strong claim opens a targeted search for independent evidence, the strongest counter-model, boundary cases, and economic consequences—even beyond the base portfolio.

  4. 04

    Confront causal models

    Thesis and antithesis must produce a new synthesis with a clear boundary and prediction, not the compromise that both are partly right.

  5. 05

    Build one argument

    Stories receive a necessary order and answer one question. A striking event without a transferable mechanism remains evidence or is cut.

  6. 06

    Verify and simplify

    Every fact returns to its original source, while headlines and opening sentences form a short, readable version of the whole issue. Russian is polished before translation.

Selection check

How we verify that the issue contains what truly matters

  1. Find the signal. A broad weekly radar surfaces candidates but neither dictates topics nor limits the research that follows.
  2. Reach the mechanism. For a strong claim, we seek independent support, the best counter-model, its boundary, and where money, risk, or scarcity moves.
  3. Build the issue. Only a synthesis that changes a decision and survives a return to the originals enters the text; its complex structure is then translated into plain language.
