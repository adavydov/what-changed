AI’s value is determined not by execution speed, but by verifiability and process design

AI tools are moving more quickly into workflows, vendor selection, and infrastructure, but value at scale emerges only where the work has been redesigned and results can be checked. Accenture’s research shows how rare that redesign is; in agentic purchasing, the final decision is also still rarely handed over to the system.

Issue 07 · 13–19 July 2026 · 10–12 min read

AI value requires process redesignAccenture reports that only a small share of companies have redesigned processes around AI tools. BCG AI Radar likewise finds that companies reporting value creation at scale are rare.
