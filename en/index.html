AI is becoming a production system

The week's central change: value is moving from a model's ability to answer to a company's ability to accept, verify, and reproduce the result safely.

Issue 01 · July 11–18, 2026 · 10–12 min read

The central shift: maturity is defined by the structure of errorsTwo systems with 70% accuracy can have opposite value in real work. The first reliably handles seven known families of tasks and predictably fails on the remaining three. The second makes random mistakes in 30% of cases across every process. The first can be routed by rules; the risk of the second cannot be controlled with a small random sample.
