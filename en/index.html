An Agent Needs a Mandate, Not Just a Name

An ordinary log sees what an agent did, but does not prove who authorized it to do what. This week, different markets began assembling the missing object: a mandate with an owner, boundaries, a term, and revocation. The model answers ‘how’; the mandate answers ‘on whose behalf—and whether it may act at all.’
