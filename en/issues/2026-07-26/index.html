Chapter 01

How production experience stays in the system

Story 01

OpenAI turns production failures into tests for the next version

OpenAI introduced Presence for voice and chat agents in large companies on July 22. Each agent receives one specific job. The company defines accessible data, approved actions, checkpoints, and the moment when a person takes over.

Before launch, simulations run common requests, dangerous cases, and rare exceptions. They check the final outcome, policy compliance, tool calls, and timely escalation. A production version follows those trials.

After launch, Presence collects sessions where an agent stalled, broke a rule, or handed the conversation to a person. Codex investigates them and proposes a change. The team tests the new version on the same cases and approves a gradual rollout.

OpenAI says its English-language phone support resolves 75% of inbound issues without an operator. Its update loop cut human handoffs by 15 percentage points in ten days.

A failure becomes a permanent test. Its record keeps the initial state, expected result, actual action, triggered policy, and escalation point. The next version must pass that case together with the existing evaluation set.

After launch, an engineer reviews fresh sessions, groups similar failures, and changes a policy or tool. Presence is currently available to eligible enterprise customers through OpenAI engineers and selected integrators.

The buyer tracks the time from a failure to a new test and sees which errors return after release. Every human handoff keeps its reason in the same record.

Story 02

CLA and Baker Tilly put their firms’ methods into AI systems

CLA started building a proprietary model for accounting services with Digits. Digits supplies the ledger platform and training technology. CLA brings the firm’s methods and the working decisions of its professionals.

The model receives new material whenever a professional reviews, categorizes, or corrects a transaction in Digits. That is how the companies describe the learning mechanism. CLA plans to deploy it to thousands of current and future clients over three years.

The first rollout goes to client accounting and advisory services. That practice contributes roughly 15% of CLA’s annual revenue of more than $2 billion and works with tens of thousands of private companies. A rare exception at one client can appear again at another.

One day earlier, Baker Tilly said Ode would design and implement systems for audit, tax, and consulting around the firm’s methods, knowledge, and professional standards.

For a group of accounting firms, scale begins with the correction record. It keeps the agent’s proposal together with the accountant’s decision and explanation. The source document, client type, rule validity period, and reviewer sit beside them.

The record states where the rule applies. A general principle may cover the whole portfolio. Contract terms and sensitive data remain tied to one client. Training and example-viewing permissions are set separately.

A new exception type first reaches a senior accountant. Several confirmed cases can become a rule with a defined scope. The decision history shows where the rule has worked and which variants returned for manual review.

When a similar transaction appears at another client, the agent shows the rule together with earlier decisions and its scope. A senior accountant confirms the new case or sends it for manual review.

How numbers, authority, and origin survive agent work

Story 03

Financial AI finds the right table and reads the wrong number

Two studies published this week caught a nasty class of errors. The agent had already opened the right report and found the table, then bound the number to a neighboring row, another period, the wrong unit, or another part of the corporate group.

FinIndices assembled 68,307 tasks from the statements of 829 public companies. Models read documents up to 32,000 tokens, selected rows, reconciled periods, and calculated metrics. Its strict grader accepted only a fully correct table.

Gemini 3.1 Pro scored 70.70% with an explicit formula. When it had to reconstruct the formula, the score was 38.22%. Models confused a quarter with year-to-date, a point-in-time balance with a period flow, and group figures with parent-company data. In a large table, the model grabbed nearby columns and literal arithmetic.

FinanceComplexQA added 2,026 questions across 1,009 real financial documents with complex layouts. Its error analysis found correct formulas paired with the wrong period, row, unit, or sign. Some answers used a group figure for a question about one segment.

Multi-step searches used 31,400 to 45,800 tokens and 22.8 to 50.6 seconds per question. LightRAG and PageIndex used 12,800 to 15,600 tokens and 8.6 to 11.9 seconds. The expensive route fits questions whose evidence is scattered across the document.

A financial agent needs an exact coordinate for each value. The record carries the document, table, row, column, period, unit, currency, sign, corporate scope, and metric definition. A page link covers only part of that trail.

Ordinary code performs the calculation from an explicit formula. The model finds coordinates, assembles the explanation, and flags uncertain choices. A checker verifies inputs and output. A long search is reserved for work that combines evidence from several parts of a report.

In an accounting product, the coordinate continues into the journal entry and reporting line. A person sees the selected period and unit before approval. A correction keeps the revised coordinate and its reason for the next test.

Story 04

The AWS MCP server kept running after its security policy failed to load

AWS API MCP Server lets an agent run AWS CLI commands. An administrator can add a policy above IAM permissions to deny an operation or require approval before execution.

The server loaded its policy data at startup. With fail-closed mode disabled, a load failure left the process running and skipped the policy check until restart. A denied or gated command could execute within the credentials’ remaining IAM permissions.

The vulnerability affected versions 0.2.13 through 1.3.46. Version 1.3.47 fixed it. GitHub gave it a 7.3 severity score.

A readiness check for an MCP server should confirm a live process and a loaded policy. Service state exposes the policy version, checksum, and successful load time.

A safe startup blocks requests after a load failure and reports the cause. A separate test deliberately breaks the policy path and expects a blocked request with an initialization error.

IAM remains an independent boundary. The agent’s role receives only the actions and resources of its current task, preferably for a short period. The MCP policy handles finer denials and approvals inside that perimeter.

A financial agent needs the same startup contract. Posting authority begins after amount limits, approved accounts, and the approval route have loaded.

Story 05

The European Commission clarified the rules for disclosing AI involvement

The European Commission published its Article 50 guidance on July 20 and a detailed FAQ on July 24. The obligations begin to apply on August 2, 2026. They separate a conversation with an agent, machine-readable content origin, and a visible label on published material.

In a direct interaction, the product discloses AI from the start of the first contact unless the nature of the system is already obvious. Background work and machine-to-machine exchange fall outside this rule. Disclosure becomes an interface state shown before the first substantive answer.

A generative-system provider adds a machine-readable mark to synthetic text, audio, images, or video. Exceptions cover source code, short character sequences, machine exchange, and parts of closed industrial production.

Visible disclosure follows a separate rule. A deepfake is disclosed by the time a person first sees it. This duty requires a clear visible or audible label.

The exemption for public-interest text requires substantive human review and editorial responsibility. The reviewer examines the meaning and accepts responsibility for publication. Spelling and grammar checks count as formal editing.

For product implementation, an item moves through internal output, draft, reviewed version, and publication states. The transition log keeps the model version, source material, machine-readable mark, and editor.

On export, the system preserves the machine-readable mark in an email, document, or image. The first contact records the disclosure. The editorial route records a visible label where required.
