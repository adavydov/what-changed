Chapter 01

How agent work reaches a result

Story 01

Eight times more code raised research output by roughly 2.5 times

Claude now writes more than 80% of the lines that enter Anthropic's main codebase. In the second quarter of 2026, a typical engineer added eight times more code per day than in 2024. On one project, Claude prepared more than 800 fixes and reduced one class of API errors by a factor of one thousand. The engineer in charge estimated four years for the manual route.

The eightfold number counts code. A researcher still chooses the hypothesis, reads the result, and decides where to go next. Thomas Kwa at METR built an economic model of the whole job. If coding once took half the time, speeding it up eightfold lifts total output by 2.83 times.

An agent writes internal utilities and tools for one-off checks especially fast. A complex change spends longer in review. When the METR model gives different kinds of code different value, the total gain falls in a range of 2.33–2.66 times. The central estimate is close to 2.5.

At Anthropic, code already arrives faster than people can review it. The flow of ideas, internal tools, and simulations has grown too, so the company continues only some of them. Everything else waits for a person to read the result and decide what happens next.

Workload rises in a similar way outside the lab. Among workers facing substantial change from AI, 69% told Deloitte that their workload had increased. For 43%, lack of time was the main barrier. Reading fresh reports and checking drafts now compete with supervising several agents.

Before an agent starts, the team records who will decide what happens to its result. The next action appears in the same record. Work with low expected value stops early, and the queue has a hard limit. An item loses priority when nobody makes a decision on it for too long.

An accounting firm can put this queue in front of a senior accountant. The agent prepares hundreds of matches and explanations, then sends up cases with monetary risk. A new type of exception also gets priority. Weak evidence sends a case to the same place; the others wait for an automatic check or the next review window.

The work screen shows the age of each case and the time until a confirmed decision. Another measure tracks the share of machine-made material that led to a record in the accounting system. Generated text counts only as load on the queue.

Story 02

Choose the model separately for each operation

EnterpriseOps-Gym-AA tests models in eight areas of office work, where they can reach 164 tables and 512 tools. An agent works through 1,117 multi-step tasks. The checker reads the final records in the work systems.

The leader at publication passed 79% of individual checks and completed 51% of whole tasks. One missed constraint erased the result. In an audit-preparation example, the agent must create a folder with exactly four files, give auditors temporary access, and move two incidents to resolved. A status of closed counts as an error.

GPT-5.5 ranked third overall and first in customer service. On Microsoft Teams tasks, it fell to 20th place out of 28. Mistral Medium 3.5 stood 18th overall and fourth on email. A customer reply therefore calls for the customer-service results. Email work gets its own ranking, as do actions inside Teams.

Cost varied by roughly 90 times. DeepSeek V4 Pro scored 40% at about one cent per task. Claude Fable 5 led with 51% at 93 cents. GLM-5.2 reached 43% for 10 cents, and Claude Opus 4.8 reached 44% for 79 cents. A buying sheet records quality and cost separately for every operation.

BCG suggests opening access step by step. First, the agent's decision is quietly compared with a person's. A person then approves every action. Familiar classes of operation open after enough tests. A specific step receives full access once it has a stable record.

A record for each operation links it to a model version. It states how the final entry will be checked and sets an acceptable cost. The current access level sits in a separate field. After a model update, all previous cases run again, and fresh results determine the new rights.

A cheap model can receive broad access for posting an ordinary bank payment after stable tests. Changing a supplier's bank details still requires a person's approval. One finance agent can give the model different limits for different operations.

The test set gradually describes the firm's real accounting practice. After a failure, the exact starting state and expected record become a new case. A new model runs through those cases before the work route changes.

Chapter 02

How to connect different accounting systems

Story 03

Different charts of accounts map into one finance language

After several acquisitions, the same revenue appears in different accounts under different codes. The sales channel may be hidden inside the account number or stored in a separate field. An experienced employee remembers the translations and builds the consolidated report by hand.

Deloitte suggests storing meaning separately from the account number. Product gets its own field, with geography beside it. Sales channel becomes another explicit feature. Hundreds of revenue accounts map to one concept with clear ways to break it down. The local chart of accounts keeps working through a mapping table.

Above that table sits a vocabulary the agent can read. Deloitte describes a finance graph that picks the right meaning from the surrounding details. It connects company reference data with supporting ledgers and the general ledger. Another link runs to the planning system. Every definition has an owner, a change history, and a path back to the source entry.

After a deal, the meaning on a particular date matters. If a business moves a product into another category in July, a March query opens the March mapping. The agent reads what the term meant at the time and shows the path from its answer to the old account.

In a group of accounting firms, the work starts by connecting each ledger. A payment receives a shared form and keeps its original identifier. The document links to the counterparty and obligation. The local rule stays beside the record, so the check reads the version that applied when the action happened.

Xero is already bringing this language into the product. The company announced one screen that shows book health and work status across a practice. It also shows readiness for close. JAX reads source documents and matches bank transactions, then looks for missing receipts. A planned feature handles a payment split between a sale and a fee.

Xero's tax calculation reads the active general ledger down to each transaction. The path remains visible from receipt to return. The XeroForce builder connects outside applications to finance data through a custom agent.

A new firm links its chart of accounts to the shared concepts and checks the mapping against previous closes. After that test, the agent reads the same financial meaning across systems. Each action returns to the source ledger through a controlled route.
